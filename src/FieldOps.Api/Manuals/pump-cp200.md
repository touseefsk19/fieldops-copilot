# CP-200 Centrifugal Pump: Maintenance Guide

Sample manual written for the FieldOps Copilot demo. Not a real vendor document.

## 1.1 Safety before work
Isolate the pump electrically and apply lockout/tagout before any maintenance. Close the suction and discharge valves and drain the casing. Wear safety glasses and gloves; the casing can stay hot for 30 minutes after shutdown.

## 2.1 Daily checks
Check for unusual noise, vibration and leaks once per shift. Bearing housing temperature must stay below 80 °C. Record the discharge pressure; normal range is 3.5 to 4.5 bar.

## 3.2 Mechanical seal leakage
A mechanical seal in good condition shows no visible leakage. More than 10 drops per minute means the seal must be replaced. Common causes are running the pump dry, misalignment, or abrasive particles in the fluid. Use seal kit SK-200.

## 4.2 Coupling bolts and alignment
Tighten the coupling bolts in a cross pattern to 45 N·m. After any motor or pump removal, check shaft alignment with a dial indicator; maximum allowed offset is 0.05 mm.

## 5.1 Vibration troubleshooting
High vibration usually comes from misalignment, a damaged impeller, worn bearings or cavitation. Cavitation sounds like gravel inside the pump; check suction pressure and make sure the suction valve is fully open.
