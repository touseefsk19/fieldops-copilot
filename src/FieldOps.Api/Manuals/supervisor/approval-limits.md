# Maintenance Approval Limits (Supervisor only)

Sample policy written for the FieldOps Copilot demo. Not a real company document.

## 1. Spend approval limits
A supervisor may approve a maintenance request up to 5,000 USD. Requests from 5,000 to 25,000 USD need the maintenance manager. Anything above 25,000 USD needs the plant manager.

## 2. Overtime approval
A supervisor may approve up to 4 hours of overtime per technician per day. More than 12 hours per week for one technician needs the maintenance manager.

## 3. Emergency work orders
In an emergency the supervisor may start work before approval, but must raise the request within 24 hours and record the reason.
