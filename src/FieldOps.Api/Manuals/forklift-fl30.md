# FL-30 Electric Forklift: Operator and Service Notes

Sample manual written for the FieldOps Copilot demo. Not a real vendor document.

## 1.1 Pre-shift inspection
Before each shift check the horn, lights, brakes, steering, forks, chains and battery charge. Do not operate the truck if any check fails; tag it out of service and report it to your supervisor.

## 2.3 Battery charging
Charge only in the marked charging area with ventilation. Charge when the battery indicator shows 20 percent or less. Never top up battery water before charging; add distilled water after charging.

## 3.4 Error code E-4021
E-4021 means the lift motor controller detected over-temperature. Lower the load, switch the truck off and let it cool for 15 minutes. If the code returns within one hour, check the cooling fan and contact maintenance.

## 4.1 Fork and chain wear
Replace a fork when the heel thickness is worn by 10 percent or more. Lift chains must be lubricated every 250 operating hours.
