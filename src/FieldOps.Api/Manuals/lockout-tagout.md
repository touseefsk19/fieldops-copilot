# Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Procedure

Sample procedure written for the FieldOps Copilot demo. Not a real company document.

## 1. Purpose
Lockout/tagout prevents equipment from starting unexpectedly while someone is working on it. It applies to every energy source: electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic and thermal.

## 2. Steps to apply LOTO
Notify affected staff. Shut the equipment down normally. Isolate every energy source. Attach your personal lock and tag to each isolation point. Release stored energy. Verify zero energy by trying to start the equipment.

## 3. Removing LOTO
Only the person who applied a lock may remove it. Check that tools are removed and guards are refitted, warn affected staff, then remove locks and tags and restore energy.
