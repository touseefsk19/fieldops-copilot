# CV-10 Belt Conveyor: Service Notes

Sample manual written for the FieldOps Copilot demo. Not a real vendor document.

## 1.1 Safety before work
Stop the conveyor and apply lockout/tagout at the drive isolator before touching the belt, rollers or guards. Never reach into a moving conveyor, and refit every guard before restarting.

## 2.1 Belt tension
Correct belt sag is 15 to 20 mm at mid-span between two idlers. A slipping belt or a squealing drive pulley means the belt is too loose; adjust both take-up screws by the same number of turns.

## 3.1 Belt tracking
If the belt runs to one side, adjust the idler on that side forward a quarter turn at a time and run the belt for two minutes between adjustments. Belt edge wear usually means tracking has been wrong for a long time.

## 4.1 Error code C-17
C-17 means the drive motor overloaded. Check for jammed cartons or a seized roller, clear the jam, wait 5 minutes for the motor to cool, then reset at the control panel. If C-17 returns within an hour, report it to your supervisor.
